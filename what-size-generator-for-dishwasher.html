What Size Generator Do I Need to Run a Dishwasher?

The two charts disagree by a wide margin. Cover the higher running figure.

Quick answer: Honda lists a cool-dry dishwasher at 216 running watts and 540 starting watts, and Champion lists a dishwasher at 1,200–2,400 running watts with no separate start. The charts disagree, so the size that covers both clears 2,400 running watts. A Honda EU2200i rated at 1,800 W, even with a 2,200 W maximum, does not cover a 2,400 W running load, while two units in parallel reach up to 4,400 W and the Champion manual lists 5,000 W running.

How to size it

Add the running watts of every load that will be on together, then add the single highest starting surge (Generac; Honda). Generac notes that starting watts are often 2–3 times running watts, and that standby units should get a dealer survey.

Do not invent a Champion start. Honda's start is 540 W, which is already under Champion's running range. If your machine matches the cool-dry row only, the EU2200i rated output of 1,800 W covers 216 W running and the maximum of 2,200 W covers 540 W starting.

FAQ

Why is Champion so much higher than Honda?

They are different published rows. Honda is a cool-dry dishwasher at 216 W running and 540 W starting. Champion is 1,200–2,400 W running with no separate start. Cover 2,400 W if you do not know which row matches the machine.

Can a Honda EU2200i run a cool-dry dishwasher?

Yes for Honda's row. 216 W running is under 1,800 W rated, and 540 W starting is under 2,200 W maximum.

Does the chart list a heated-dry surge?

No. Use the listed figures only. Champion does not list a separate start at all.

What if the washer is on the same generator?

Add the washer's running watts, then keep the higher start. A washer start can be 2,300 W, and the dishwasher running load can be 2,400 W. The generator has to cover the combination, not just one row.
