What GeneratorSizer is for

Generator sizing calculator for homeowners who need to know exactly what wattage keeps the house running during an outage.

Not a commercial paralleling-switchgear manual. Transfer-switch work is licensed-electrician territory.

This map lists pages that exist. It is not a keyword dump and it does not treat every article as its own niche. The calculator (or the pack) is the hub. Guides sit under one pillar each.
