Questions buyers actually ask

Straight answers, no marketing fog.

What hardware do I need to run it?

The Q4_K_M GGUF is about 4.4 GB. It runs comfortably in 8 GB of unified memory (M1/M2/M3 Mac mini, modern Mini-PC) or on any 8 GB GPU. Q8_0 is roughly 8 GB and wants a 12 GB card. CPU-only also works, just slower.

Is any of my data sent to a server?

No. The model files run locally through llama.cpp, Ollama, or TabbyAPI on your own hardware. The only network traffic is the Whop download and optional email support.

Can I re-export the model to another format?

Yes. The bundle ships a Q4_K_M GGUF that runs directly in llama.cpp, Ollama, or TabbyAPI. The README covers re-quantising to Q5_K_M or Q8_0 if you want more precision.

Are there other editions or tiers?

No. There is one edition: the fine-tuned Qwen2.5-7B model bundle at $99. Apache-2.0, yours to keep, no subscription and no higher tier to upgrade into.
