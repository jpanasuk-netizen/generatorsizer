How to use this with the calculator

Add a row on the homepage calculator for each device you will run at the same time. A card-only or CPU-only figure is not the whole computer. One example from this table: the NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5090 card is 575 W and the Mac mini (M6) is 70 W, so those two together are 645 W before anything else. The appliance chart and the portable generator watts chart are separate tables. A battery comparison is on generator vs battery backup, and a small continuous load example is TV and router.
