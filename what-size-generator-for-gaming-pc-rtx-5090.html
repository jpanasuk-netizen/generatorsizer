What Size Generator for a Gaming PC RTX 5090

Published November 10, 2026.

The card wattage and the whole-PC power figure are different numbers. Size the generator from the whole PC, then add the monitor.

Quick answer: NVIDIA lists the GeForce RTX 5090 at 575 watts of total graphics power and calls for a 1,000 watt power supply for the whole PC. Size the generator to that 1,000 W figure plus your monitor. Honda's EU2200i, rated at 1,800 running watts, covers it with about 800 W to spare.

The 800 W spare is 1,800 minus 1,000: the EU2200i rated output minus NVIDIA's required system power. It does not subtract a monitor. Those two figures are still the ones printed on the pages linked above. The same NVIDIA page also prints a separate 850 W minimum in its Founders Edition checklist, quoted in the table below. This page sizes from the specs-table 1,000 W figure.

Size the generator

The form starts on the RTX 5090 picklist row (575 W) and on NVIDIA's 1,000 W required system power. Add a monitor, a router, or both. The running load is the PC watts plus those add-ons. Nothing on the cited pages lists a motor start for this PC, so peak watts equal that running load.

Headroom starts at 20%, the same default the inverter generator page uses for the homepage calculator. The recommendation rounds up to the next 500 W. That percent and that rounding are this site's calculator rule, not a figure on NVIDIA's or Honda's page.

Champion's wattage chart lists an internet router/modem at 10–20 W and says those appliances do not require extra starting watts. The router field starts at 0. Type a number in that 10–20 W band to add one. A monitor is not part of the 1,000 W PC figure. The monitor field also starts at 0; enter watts from the monitor's label.
