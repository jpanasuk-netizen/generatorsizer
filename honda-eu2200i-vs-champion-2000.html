FAQ

Which one has more power?

On paper the Honda. Honda lists 1,800 W rated and 2,200 W maximum. Champion lists the 100705 at 1,600 W running and 2,000 W starting. That is 200 W more on each line for the Honda.

Which Champion 2000 is this?

Model 100705, the gasoline 2000W Inverter. Champion's 2000W dual-fuel model 201230 lists 1,700 running watts on gasoline and 1,530 on propane, so check the model number before you compare.

Which is quieter?

They are listed differently. Champion gives 53 dBA from 23 feet. The Honda figure used on this site is 48–57 dB(A). The two listings do not state the same test, so they are not a direct match.

Can either run a 2,000 W starting load?

Both list a starting or maximum figure of at least 2,000 W: 2,200 W for the EU2200i and 2,000 W for the 100705. The checker on this page compares your own running and starting watts against both.
