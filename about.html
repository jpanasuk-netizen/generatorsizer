About GeneratorSizer

Jeremy Panasuk, PE

Who I Am

Jeremy Panasuk is a licensed engineer (PE) who spent 19 years recovering critical data for Fortune 100 companies. Today, he applies that same precision to helping homeowners and contractors properly size backup power systems.

Why This Tool Exists

After seeing families lose thousands of dollars to improperly sized generators, I built a tool based on actual field measurements, not guesswork. Every calculation follows:

  • NEC Article 220 - National Electrical Code standards
  • Manufacturer nameplate data - Real-world appliance specifications
  • DOE/EIA energy data - Government-standardized fuel costs

Your Trust Matters

I personally review every major update and read every email. Your questions help improve this tool for everyone. Before you buy a generator, you should know: oversized units waste fuel; undersized units fail when you need them most.
