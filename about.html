Why the calculators exist

Most generator sizing content online is a blog post padded around an affiliate link: pick a wattage class from a feel, round up generously, done. That's how people end up with a generator that stalls when the well pump kicks in, or a 12,000 W machine idling for a fridge and a phone charger. GeneratorSizer exists to do the opposite — you enter the actual appliances you plan to run, and the tool computes running watts, startup surge, 20% headroom, runtime at 50% load, and gasoline vs propane daily fuel consumption from those inputs.

The Lab tools go further than that first-pass sum: staggered-start coincidence, altitude/heat derate, a printable 72-hour runbook, five-year portable-vs-standby cost, and lot-line noise. Those pages are the unique work. The method is written out on Our math.

What the numbers follow

  • NEC Article 220 — branch-circuit, feeder, and service load calculations (Article 120 in the 2026 NEC). Method context, not a license to design your panel
  • Manufacturer nameplate / typical watts — editable in the calculator
  • EIA sample heat content — motor gasoline 120,214 Btu/gal, propane 91,452 Btu/gal
  • CPSC placement — outdoors only, at least 20 ft from the house
