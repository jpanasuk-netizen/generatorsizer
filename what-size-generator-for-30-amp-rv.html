What Size Generator Do I Need for a 30 Amp RV?

Published October 20, 2026.

A 30 amp RV is limited by its shore power cord. Match that limit, then check the air conditioner's start.

Full breakdown: RV generator sizing.

Quick answer: Cummins says a 30 amp RV on 120 volts can handle about 3,600 watts (30 × 120), so a 3,600 W generator matches shore power and 4,000 W adds starting room. Honda lists a 13,500 BTU RV air conditioner at 1,800 running and 2,800 starting watts. Westinghouse's WGen3600 has a TT-30R outlet and 3,600 running watts.

How to size it

Start with the cord. Cummins multiplies volts by amps: 120 × 30 is about 3,600 W, the most the coach can draw on a 30 amp hookup. A generator rated 3,600 running watts gives you the same power as the pedestal.

Then check the start. The roof air conditioner is usually the biggest motor. Honda lists a 13,500 BTU unit at 2,800 W to start and 1,800 W to run. A 15,000 BTU unit is larger; see the 15,000 BTU RV air conditioner page, and the single-unit detail is on the 13,500 BTU page.

Add the other loads that run with it, then size from the total. Running wattages for common appliances are in the portable generator watts chart, and the generator size calculator adds them for you.

Look for a TT-30R outlet so the RV cord plugs straight in. The WGen3600 is one listed example. For tent or van trips with no air conditioner, see the best portable generator for camping.

FAQ

Why 3,600 watts for a 30 amp RV?

Watts are volts times amps. Cummins uses 120 V × 30 A, which is about 3,600 W, the most a 30 amp shore connection can carry.

Is 4,000 watts better than 3,600?

Cummins says a 4,000 W generator gives additional motor-starting power. That matters most when the roof air conditioner starts.

What is the smallest generator that can run the air conditioner?

Cummins calls 2,500 to 2,800 W a good minimum for 30 amp RVs with air conditioning. Honda lists a 13,500 BTU RV air conditioner at 2,800 starting watts.

What plug does a 30 amp RV use?

A TT-30 plug. The Westinghouse WGen3600 is one example with a TT-30R outlet on the panel.
