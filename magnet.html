The outage cheat sheet

Fridge. Well pump. Furnace fan. That stack decides whether you bought a generator or an expensive night-light.

Most sizing posts add every appliance in the house. Motors do not all start at once. Only the biggest surge counts on top of everything already running. Then apply the headroom you set on the calculator.

How to use it. Circle the loads you will actually run. Add running watts from the nameplate. Add the single largest starting-watt extra. Apply the calculator's headroom. That is the class to shop, not the brochure slogan.
