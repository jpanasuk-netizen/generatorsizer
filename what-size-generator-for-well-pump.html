What Size Generator Do I Need to Run a Well Pump?

Published October 2, 2026.

Horsepower changes the start by a wide margin. Read the row that matches the pump.

Full breakdown: well pump watts.

Longer size guide: which generator size fits a well pump.

Quick answer: Champion lists a 1/2 hp well pump at 750–1,000 running watts and 1,500–2,000 starting watts, and a 1 hp well pump at 1,000–2,000 running watts and 2,000–4,000 starting watts. Covering the 1 hp row takes a running rating that clears 2,000 W and a starting rating that clears 4,000 W. A single Honda EU2200i, rated 1,800 W with a 2,200 W maximum, covers the 1/2 hp row but not the 1 hp row, while two EU2200i units in parallel reach up to 4,400 W and the Champion manual lists 5,000 W running and 6,250 W starting.

How to size it

Add the running watts of every load that will be on together, then add the single highest starting surge (Generac; Honda). Generac notes that starting watts are often 2–3 times running watts, and that standby units should get a dealer survey.

Honda's wattage guide does not list a well pump, so this page does not borrow one. A hardwired pump needs a transfer switch or interlock installed by an electrician. See the transfer switch sizing guide.

FAQ

What size generator for a 1/2 hp well pump?

Champion lists 750–1,000 W running and 1,500–2,000 W starting. The EU2200i rated output is 1,800 W and the maximum is 2,200 W, which covers that row.

What size generator for a 1 hp well pump?

Clear 2,000 W running and 4,000 W starting. Parallel EU2200i output is up to 4,400 W. The manual lists 5,000 W running and 6,250 W starting.

Does a single EU2200i cover a 1 hp pump?

No. The pump can draw 2,000 W running, above the 1,800 W rated output, and 4,000 W starting, above the 2,200 W maximum.
