A tri-fuel unit can run from the home's natural gas line, which is why people choose it when gasoline deliveries stop. Propane and natural gas ratings are often lower than the gasoline rating. Read those three ratings on the sheet and size to the fuel you will actually burn.

10. Best near-standby portable

The large dual-fuel class is the portable ceiling: it feeds a high-amp switch and covers most of a house short of electric heat, if the calculator says so. Fuel burn at the load you will run is on the spec sheet. Price a dual-fuel setup from that sheet, not from a gallons-per-hour guess.

How the ranking was made

Every slot is a class, not a single model, because inventory rotates. Within each class, compare four figures on the current manufacturer sheet: surge watts (they must clear your worst startup — well pump owners, read this first), runtime at the load you will actually run, total harmonic distortion if the load is electronic, and 240 V output if you have a well pump or want transfer-switch wiring. Ratings drift between revisions.

Bought the generator? Two accessories that matter more than the upgrade

A manual transfer switch or interlock kit, which code requires for a panel connection, and an outdoor cord rated for the load.
