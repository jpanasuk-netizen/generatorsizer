How generator sizing works

Three numbers decide what you buy. Running watts — the sum of everything switched on at the same time. Starting watts — the 2–4× spike when a motor kicks on; only the largest one counts at any instant, because motors don't all start together. Headroom — generators hate running flat-out; at 100% load they run hot, loud, and short-lived, and fuel burn climbs faster than output. The 20% margin keeps you in the efficient zone and leaves room for the appliance you forgot.
