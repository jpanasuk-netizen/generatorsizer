What size generator for a heat pump?

Quick answer: Goodman's spec sheet lists its 3-ton GSZH5 heat pump (GSZH503610) compressor at 16.0 running amps and 91.9 locked-rotor amps on 208/230 V single-phase, with a 22.8 A minimum circuit ampacity and a 35 A max breaker. The 2-ton model is 11.5 and 59.5 A, and the 5-ton is 25.6 and 151.0 A.

What the numbers mean for a generator

RLA (rated load amps) is the compressor's running current. LRA (locked-rotor amps) is the current it can pull for a moment as it starts, before the motor is turning. The generator has to carry the starting moment, not just the running amps, on top of everything else that is already on.

These are 208/230 V units. A generator or inverter that only puts out 120 V can't run them at all, no matter its wattage. You need a 240 V output and a transfer switch rated for the circuit. See transfer switch sizing.

Many heat pumps also have electric backup heat strips in the indoor unit. Those are a separate, large load and are not in this table. Check your air handler's nameplate.
