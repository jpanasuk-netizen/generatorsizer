Privacy & Legal

Short version: no tracking, no cookies, no data leaves your browser.

What we collect

Calculator inputs stay in your browser tab — nothing you type into the calculators is transmitted. Amazon links are affiliate links (we earn from qualifying purchases); they carry no extra tracking beyond Amazon's own. Google AdSense auto ads may set advertising cookies; you can opt out at google.com/settings/ads. Two forms transmit data: the installer-quote form (name, ZIP, phone, sized-kW) and the printable-checklist opt-in (email) are delivered via FormSubmit to the site operator for the sole purpose of quoting/contacting you. We never sell or share submissions. GitHub Pages logs standard access data on their side; we don't read it per-visitor.

Affiliate disclosure

Some pages may contain affiliate links. If you buy through one, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Affiliate links never change the sizing math or a ranking position — products that don't fit the computed load don't get recommended. Affiliate links carry Amazon's Associates tag and never change the sizing math or a ranking position.

Advertising

This site serves ads through Google AdSense (publisher ID ca-pub-1045858506533973). The AdSense script loads on our pages and Google and its partners may set advertising cookies to personalize the ads you see. You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings or via aboutads.info. Ad slots never change the sizing math or a recommendation — the same rules apply to advertising as to affiliate links.
