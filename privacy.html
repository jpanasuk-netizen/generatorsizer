Privacy & Legal

Short version: no tracking, no cookies, no data leaves your browser.

What we collect

Nothing today. All calculator inputs stay in your browser tab — there is no server, no analytics script, and no cookie on this site. GitHub Pages serves static files and logs standard access data on their side; we don't read it per-visitor. The installer-quote form is a draft: it submits nowhere and stores nothing until a vetted operator program launches (see the staging note on the form itself).

Affiliate disclosure

Some pages may contain affiliate links. If you buy through one, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Affiliate links never change the sizing math or a ranking position — products that don't fit the computed load don't get recommended. Staged Amazon links currently carry no Associate ID and earn nothing; tracking activates only after program approval.

Advertising (staged)

Ad slots on the site are placeholders. No ad network script loads and no publisher ID is configured, so no ad profiling occurs. If display ads activate later, this page will name the network.
