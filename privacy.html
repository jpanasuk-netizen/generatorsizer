What this site loads, and what it does not.

What we collect

Calculator inputs stay in your browser. assets/app.js and assets/lab.js compute results on the page. They do not send what you type into a calculator to a server, and they do not set cookies.

Two optional forms on the homepage (and the same forms on upload-index.html) submit only if you send them. They post to formsubmit.co, which emails the form fields to jpanasuk@gmail.com. The installer form sends the name, ZIP code, phone number, and the sized-kW field. The checklist form sends the email address you type.

assets/monetization.js rewrites Amazon search links in the browser so they use the affiliate tag generatorsi0d-20. assets/magnet-cta.js, where a page loads it, adds a link to the printable cheat sheet and a TikTok link. Neither of those files loads analytics.

Cloudflare, which sits in front of this site, adds two scripts to the HTML it serves. One rewrites the contact email so the address is not plain text in the response, then loads an email-decode script to show it. The other is the Cloudflare Insights beacon at static.cloudflareinsights.com. Those scripts are not files in this repository.

Affiliate disclosure

Some pages contain Amazon affiliate links (tag generatorsi0d-20). As an Amazon Associate, we earn from qualifying purchases. If you buy through one, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Those links do not change the sizing math. When you follow an Amazon link, Amazon's own site may set cookies under Amazon's policy.

Advertising

This site does not load Google AdSense or any other advertising script. No ads run on these pages. An ads.txt file is present; it does not place ads by itself.
