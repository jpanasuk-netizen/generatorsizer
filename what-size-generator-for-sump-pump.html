What Size Generator Do I Need to Run a Sump Pump?

Size the start from both charts, then see whether one small inverter's maximum covers it.

Full breakdown: sump pump watts.

Quick answer: Honda lists a 1/2 hp sump pump at 1,050 running watts and 2,150 starting watts, and Champion lists the same size at 800–1,050 running watts and 1,300–2,150 starting watts. Covering both takes a running rating that clears 1,050 W and a starting rating that clears 2,150 W. A Honda EU2200i is rated 1,800 W with a 2,200 W maximum, which covers that 1/2 hp start.

How to size it

Add the running watts of every load that will be on together, then add the single highest starting surge (Generac; Honda). Generac notes that starting watts are often 2–3 times running watts, and that standby units should get a dealer survey.

A 1/3 hp pump is lighter. Honda lists 800 running watts and 1,300 starting watts. Champion lists 500–800 running watts and 1,000–1,600 starting watts. If a fridge is also on the generator, add the fridge's running watts and keep only the higher start.

FAQ

What size generator for a 1/2 hp sump pump?

Clear 1,050 W running and 2,150 W starting. The EU2200i maximum is 2,200 W and the rated output is 1,800 W.

What about a 1/3 hp sump pump?

Honda lists 800 W running and 1,300 W starting. Champion lists 500–800 W running and 1,000–1,600 W starting.

Do Honda and Champion agree on the 1/2 hp start?

The top of both ranges is 2,150 W. Champion's full start range is 1,300–2,150 W, and the running range is 800–1,050 W.

Is this the whole sump guide?

No. This page is the sizing question only. The longer wattage guide is linked at the top. The 1/2 hp start to cover is still 2,150 W.
