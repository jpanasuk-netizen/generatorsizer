#### Wire Gauge & Voltage Drop
> *“I need to run a 100A subpanel 250 feet away with copper wire at 240V single phase. What wire gauge keeps voltage drop under 3%?”*

#### HVAC Load Calculations (Manual J)

#### Ambient Temperature Derating
> *“My generator runs at 105°F ambient. Rated 22kW standby. How much can it actually deliver?”*

#### Transfer Switch Sizing

#### Fuel Economics

#### NEC Ampacity & Derating

#### Grounding & Bonding

#### Standby vs Prime Power
